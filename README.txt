Before publishing:
1. Verify all insurance services offered and any required licenses/authorizations.
2. Confirm business address, email, official insurer/partner details, privacy notice and terms.
3. Review Malayalam copy and service descriptions.
4. Check domain availability through an accredited domain registrar before purchase.
5. Host the site on a web-hosting provider and connect the registered domain.

This is a website prototype, not a live/published website. It does not issue policies or calculate premiums. Coverage, premiums, eligibility and claims are subject to insurer terms and policy wording.
